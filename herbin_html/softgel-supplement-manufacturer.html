Frequently Asked Questions

Everything about softgel manufacturing and technology

What are softgel capsules?

Softgel capsules are hermetically sealed, gelatin-based capsules filled with liquid or semi-solid formulations. They provide excellent protection for sensitive ingredients and superior bioavailability.

What are the advantages of softgels over tablets?

Softgels offer better bioavailability, faster absorption, protection from oxidation, and the ability to encapsulate oils and heat-sensitive ingredients. They also provide a more professional appearance and are easier to swallow.

What types of ingredients work best in softgels?

Softgels are ideal for oil-based supplements, fat-soluble vitamins, essential fatty acids, and heat-sensitive ingredients. They're perfect for fish oil, vitamin D, vitamin E, and herbal extracts in oil form.

Do you offer vegetarian softgel options?

Yes, we provide both gelatin and vegetarian softgels made from plant-based materials. Our vegetarian capsules are suitable for vegan, halal, and kosher products.

What customization options are available?

We offer customization in size, shape, color, imprint, and formulation. Softgels can be oval, round, or custom-shaped, and we can match your brand colors and add custom imprints.

How do you ensure softgel quality?

Our softgels undergo rigorous testing for uniformity, dissolution, leakage, and content accuracy. We use advanced encapsulation technology and maintain strict quality control throughout the manufacturing process.
