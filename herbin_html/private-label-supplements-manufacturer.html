Frequently Asked Questions

Expert answers about private label supplement manufacturing

What is private label supplement manufacturing?

Private label manufacturing allows brands to create their own supplement products using our manufacturing expertise and facilities. You own the brand and marketing, while we handle the formulation, production, and quality control.

How long does it take to launch a private label product?

Typical timelines are 8-12 weeks from concept to market-ready product. This includes formulation development (2-4 weeks), regulatory documentation (2-3 weeks), and manufacturing (4-6 weeks).

What are the cost advantages of private labeling?

Private labeling eliminates the need for expensive manufacturing equipment and facilities. You benefit from our economies of scale while maintaining complete control over branding and pricing.

Can I use my existing formulation?

Yes, we can manufacture your existing formulation or help optimize it for better efficacy and manufacturability. Our R&D team can also develop entirely new formulations based on your requirements.

What quality standards do you maintain?

We maintain GMP certification, ISO 9001:2015 compliance, and FDA registration. Every batch undergoes rigorous testing, and we provide complete documentation for regulatory compliance.

How do I get started with private label manufacturing?

Contact us with your product vision, target market, and brand requirements. We'll provide a detailed proposal including formulation options, packaging designs, timelines, and pricing.
