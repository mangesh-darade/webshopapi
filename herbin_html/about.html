Who We Are

Established in 1994, Herbinn Micro Medicine is a pioneer in the manufacturing, supply, and export of nutraceutical soft gelatin capsules. We have built a legacy of trust by consistently delivering high-quality healthcare solutions.

Our products are processed using only pure, unadulterated ingredients and scientifically balanced compositions. This meticulous approach ensures maximum effectiveness and safety for our global clientele, with a primary focus on wellness without side effects.
