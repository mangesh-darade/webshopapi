Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our nutraceutical manufacturing services

What supplement formulations do you offer?

We specialize in softgel capsules, gummies, and custom formulations. Each formulation has unique benefits for different types of supplements and target markets.

When should I choose tablets over capsules?

Capsules are better for oil-based supplements, heat-sensitive ingredients, and when you want to mask unpleasant tastes.

Do you offer vegan capsule options?

Yes, we provide both gelatine and vegetarian capsule options. Our vegetarian capsules are made from plant-based materials and are suitable for vegan products.

What quality standards do you maintain for different formulations?

All our formulations meet GMP standards with rigorous quality control. We ensure consistent fill weights, proper sealing, and stability testing for every batch regardless of the formulation chosen.
