What types of products do you manufacture?

We manufacture a wide range of nutraceutical products including vitamins, minerals, herbal supplements, specialty formulations, and custom blends in various Formulas like softgels, tablets, and gummies.

Can you create custom formulations?

Yes! Our R&D team specializes in developing custom formulations based on your specific requirements. We can create unique blends that meet your target market needs and competitive positioning.

How do you ensure product quality?

Every product undergoes rigorous testing in our GMP-certified laboratory. We perform 100% batch testing, stability studies, and maintain comprehensive quality control documentation.

What certifications do your products have?

Our products meet GMP, ISO 9001:2015, FDA, and WHO-GMP standards. We can also accommodate additional certifications like halal, kosher, and organic upon request.

Do you offer sample products?

Yes, we can provide samples for evaluation and testing. Sample availability depends on the specific formulation and regulatory requirements for your market.

How do I choose the right product for my brand?

We help you select or develop products based on market research, consumer trends, regulatory requirements, and your brand positioning. Our experts guide you through the entire process.
