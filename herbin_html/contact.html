Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about working with Herbinn

How do I start a project with Herbinn?

Simply fill out our contact form or call us at +91 88795 92013. We'll schedule a consultation to understand your requirements and provide a detailed project proposal.

What information do you need to provide a quote?

We need details about your product vision, target market, formulation preferences, packaging requirements, and estimated order quantities to provide an accurate quote.

How long does the process take?

Project timelines vary, but typically include 1-2 weeks for initial consultation and quoting, 2-4 weeks for formulation development, and 4-8 weeks for manufacturing and delivery.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes! We serve clients worldwide, including North America, Europe, SE Asia, and MENA regions. We handle all export documentation and regulatory requirements for international shipping.

What is your minimum order quantity?

Our minimum order quantities vary by product type, typically ranging from 10,000-50,000 units. We can discuss smaller quantities for initial market testing.

How do you ensure product quality?

Every batch undergoes rigorous testing in our GMP-certified laboratory. We maintain comprehensive quality control documentation and provide certificates of analysis for all products.
